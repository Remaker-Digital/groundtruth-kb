# Reasoning and current decisions

Optional reasoning notes can explain alternatives and useful background.
Current formal records and project/work-item state determine what to do.
Retrieve those records through the native CLI when starting or continuing work.

Apply an owner choice directly to its affected canonical source or action.
The interactive session log retains the original conversation for later harvest
when needed. A reasoning archive does not authorize work, establish a role,
supply a GO or prove that a mutation was approved.

Do not require a deliberation search, citation or archive entry to propose,
review, implement or verify work. Preserve formal history when changing current
intent. Report any unresolved material choice to the owner before dependent work.
