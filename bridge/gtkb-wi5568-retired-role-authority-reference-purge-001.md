Retirement proposal for closing or retiring stale governed work.

## Claim

`GOV-SESSION-ROLE-AUTHORITY-001` v5 encoded a harness-scoped role-authority
model that has now produced same-harness, cross-session role interference. The
owner explicitly directed retirement and active-reference removal on
2026-07-24. The formal record is already v6 `retired`; this proposal removes
every remaining active citation mechanically while preserving canonical audit
history.

## Retirement Rationale

Role belongs to a session context or an explicitly composed dispatched worker
packet. A harness has identity, capabilities, and dispatch eligibility, but it
does not own the operating role of all concurrent sessions using that harness.
Continuing to cite the retired governance specification in startup rules,
manifests, tests, or formal specifications would keep defective authority live
despite the retirement.

This is a reference and authority cleanup, not a rewrite of historical
evidence. Existing bridge files, deliberations, prior specification/work-item
versions, and formal approval packets remain intact and may continue to name
the retired record as historical evidence.

## Risks / Rollback

The main risk is syntactic damage from deleting a token inside YAML/TOML lists
or prose lists. The transformation map therefore accepts only known preimages,
writes atomically, and aborts before mutation on any mismatch. A second risk is
silently leaving authority in MemBase while files look clean; current-spec and
current-work-item queries are mandatory independent checks.

Rollback is limited to this proposal's exact files and newly appended MemBase
versions. Existing unrelated worktree changes must not be reverted. Historical
audit records are never rollback targets.
