# DCL-SERVICE-SOT-WATCHDOG-RESTORATION-SAFETY-001 — Watchdog Restoration Safety Constraint

Derived from: ADR-PLATFORM-SERVICE-SOT-WATCHDOG-001

## Constraint
The watchdog's restoration behavior MUST satisfy:
1. Tiered classification — each restore_action is safe (idempotent: daemon restart, regenerable-index rebuild, optional-dep reinstall, cache refresh) or canonical (touches an authoritative/append-only store). Only safe actions may auto-execute.
2. Canonical fail-loud — a canonical-tier restore, or any restore failing after N attempts, MUST escalate (bridge ADVISORY plus flagged state) and MUST NOT auto-mutate a canonical store from backup.
3. Visibility-only override — components owner-marked visibility-only (precedent DELIB-20266140 / WI-4804) MUST NOT auto-restore, even if safe.
4. Probe-before-restore — a restore MUST be gated on a fresh availability probe of the target, not an inferred or cached diagnosis.
5. Resource-bounded — a heavy restore MUST run inside a whole-process-tree resource cap (Windows Job Object / cgroup), not a per-PID cap.
6. Symptom-based success — health MUST be judged by a failure-symptom probe (can the dependent critical operation proceed), not an aggregate-load threshold alone.

## Assertions
Machine-checkable checks, specified-stage (expected-fail until the watchdog lands; made concrete at implementation):
- restore-action registry declares a tier (safe|canonical) per action.
- no canonical-tier action appears in the auto-execute set.
- a resource-cap / Job-Object wrapper guards heavy restores.
- a visibility-only exclusion list is consulted before auto-restore.
