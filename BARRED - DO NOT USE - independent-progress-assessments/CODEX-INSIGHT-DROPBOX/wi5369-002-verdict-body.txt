## Blocking Finding [P1] — Undisclosed violation of the proposal's own declared parallel-session-isolation fail-closed condition

See Evidence items 2-4. Evidence captured by implementing and testing right
now would be evidence against a moving target: WI-5400's uncommitted
changes could still be revised before reaching VERIFIED, and a working-tree
pytest run exercises whatever bytes are currently on disk, not committed
HEAD. This is not a rejection of the underlying idea — the gap it targets
is real and the test design mirrors an established working precedent — it
is a request to make the proposal honest about, and resilient to, its own
declared precondition.

**Recommended action.** Either (a) explicitly sequence implementation after
WI-5400 reaches VERIFIED and is committed (mirroring how the proposal
already correctly sequences after WI-5255), naming that as an explicit
precondition; or (b) add an acceptance-criterion step requiring the
implementer to confirm `git status --porcelain` is clean on the relevant
files immediately before writing the new test, and to re-run the full
suite again after WI-5400/WI-5427 reach terminal state.
